THE BRIEF
- Audience: this site is for LCR Carpentry, a residential carpentry business. They do custom built-ins, staircases, and interior woodwork, and take on other carpentry work too. Visitors are homeowners deciding whether to trust a craftsman with their home. 

- One goal: every page should push the visitor toward calling [PHONE NUMBER]. Use one main call to action, repeated, not five competing ones. On mobile, keep a tap-to-call button persistently visible.

- Sections I want, each one should be a Svelte component <section>:
Hero: a strong headline, one line about what they build, the call button, and one standout project photo.
Services: three primary services (custom built-ins, staircases, interior woodwork), plus a short line covering other carpentry work.
Featured work: a gallery of finished projects, organized by service type, with photos as the main visual content.
Service area: where they work Carmel, Fishers, and Westfield (Indiana).
Final call to action and footer: the phone number, service area, license/insurance info if applicable.

- Vibe: warm, crafted, and confident, like a high-end woodworking studio rather than a contractor template. Think natural wood tones, generous whitespace, large photography, and editorial typography with a strong serif or characterful display face. Motion is restrained: subtle scroll reveals and image transitions that feel deliberate, nothing bouncy or flashy. Copy is plain-spoken and direct, short sentences, no buzzwords, and it lets the work speak.
